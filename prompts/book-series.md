# Book series

Many books are part of a series. For example https://www.bokus.com/bok/9789100154981/utmarker is the first part of the book series named "Sam Berger och Molly Blom" ("Del 1 i serien Sam Berger och Molly Blom") and https://www.bokus.com/bok/9789100162269/inland is the second part of the same series ("Del 2 i serien Sam Berger och Molly Blom"). 

In adlibris.com the Book Hildur (https://www.adlibris.com/fi/kirja/hildur-9789137506579) is part 1 ("Sarja:Hildur-serien (osa 01)") and the book Tinna (https://www.adlibris.com/fi/kirja/tinna-9789137164328) is part 5 ("Sarja:Hildur-serien (osa 05)").

Grouping the books by the series helps me to browse and navigate the contents.
